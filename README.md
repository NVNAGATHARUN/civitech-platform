# 🇮🇳 CitizenDesk: Empowering Every Indian with Welfare Rights

**CitizenDesk** is a revolutionary, full-stack GovTech platform designed to bridge the "Trust Gap" and "Last Mile" friction in Indian welfare distribution. Built for the modern citizen, it transforms government static data into proactive, actionable, and personalized life-support.

## 🚀 The Vision
In India, millions of eligible citizens miss out on life-changing benefits every year due to complex paperwork, language barriers, and lack of awareness. **CitizenDesk** solves this by putting an AI-powered welfare consultant in the pocket of every citizen.

## 🏆 The "Final Boss" Feature Suite (Built from Scratch)

### 1. 🧬 Automated Eligibility Engine
A custom-built matching algorithm that parses complex citizen profiles (Income, Age, Caste, Occupation, State) and filters them against a robust database of 50+ central and state schemes in real-time.

### 2. 🤖 Sahayak Chatbot (2026 Model Cluster)
A multilingual AI assistant powered by a unique **Fallback Cluster** of **Gemini 2.5, 2.0, and 1.5 models**. It provides empathetic, context-aware guidance in **Hindi, Telugu, and English**, ensuring 100% uptime for citizens.

### 3. ⚡ Welfare Intelligence Feed
A proactive dashboard inspired by social media news feeds. It pushes personalized alerts about scholarship deadlines, new farming grants, or policy changes based specifically on the user's "Citizen DNA".

### 4. 📄 Smart Eligibility Reports (PDF)
A high-impact document generator that creates branded PDF reports. Each report includes a personalized eligibility summary, a color-coded document checklist, and a secure QR verification token for officials.

### 5. ⚖️ Technical Scheme Comparator
A first-of-its-kind side-by-side analysis tool. Citizens can compare loan interest rates, grant amounts, and duration between multiple schemes to choose the best support for their family's financial future.

### 6. 📍 Last-Mile Office Locator (CSC Mapping)
Bridges the digital-physical gap by identifying the nearest **Common Service Center (CSC)** using PIN-code-based filtering for high-accuracy local guidance.

### 7. 🚨 Scheme Expiry & Priority Tracker
A smart urgency system that visualizes benefit deadlines. Using dynamic date-arithmetic, it tracks exactly how many days are left to apply, prioritizing schemes that are closing soon.

### 8. 👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 Multi-Beneficiary Hub (Household Sync)
Allows a single user to manage welfare eligibility for their entire household. Add family members to find family-based benefits like Ration Cards or Housing Grants.

### 9. 🔍 Local Document OCR Verifier
Integrated **Tesseract.js** OCR engine. Citizens can scan their physical certificates (Income/Caste) to automatically verify document details against scheme requirements without manual data entry.

### 10. 🛡️ Secure Eligibility Vault
A dedicated UI for citizens to organize their digital verification tokens and tracking history, ensuring they are always "Ready for Welfare."

### 11. 🧭 Interactive Feature Tour
A comprehensive, guided onboarding experience powered by **driver.js**. It hand-holds first-time users through the platform to ensure accessibility.

### 12. 📊 Public Impact & Demand Analytics
A dual-layer administrative view featuring:
- **India Pulse Heatmap**: Visualizing national eligibility trends.
- **Demand Heatmap**: Identifying specific districts where scheme demand is highest.

### 13. 🌉 Regional Welfare Bridge
An analytical module that visualizes the "Demand vs Supply" gap across states, helping administrators identify underserved regions that need more grassroots support.

### 14. 📲 WhatsApp Results Sharing
Integrated sharing portal that formats eligibility summaries and document lists into professional, mobile-friendly messages for parents, volunteers, or local officials.

### 15. 🔑 Secure Verification Tokens
A unique digital token system. Every verified citizen receives a tamper-proof alphanumeric token that proves eligibility status instantly during applications.

### 16. 📧 Smart Mail Sender Service
Integrated **Nodemailer** infrastructure that powers reactive communication. It sends automated notifications to citizens when they shortlist a scheme or when new schemes are added.

### 17. 🌐 Zero-Barrier Multilingual Architecture
A custom i18n implementation providing native experiences in **Hindi and Telugu**, with localized UI components and RTL-aware alignment logic.

## 🎯 Technical Innovation: The Fallback Cluster
To ensure 100% reliability, we implemented a **Model Transition Logic**. If the primary AI engine encounters region-locks or rate limits, the platform automatically cycles through 6 different Gemini models (2.5, 2.0, 1.5, etc.) in milliseconds, ensuring the citizen is never left without a "Sahayak".

## 🛠️ Tech Stack
-   **Frontend**: Next.js 16 (App Router) + React 19
-   **AI Intelligence**: Google Gemini API (2.5/2.0 Next-Gen Line)
-   **Backend/Auth**: Firebase Firestore & Firebase Authentication
-   **Visualization**: D3.js + Recharts
-   **OCR**: Tesseract.js (Client-side vision)
-   **Tour**: Driver.js
-   **Email**: Nodemailer (Serverless API Routes)
